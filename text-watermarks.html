The Monopoly Die vs. The Digits of $\pi$

In their official communication explaining the watermark, Anthropic provides an intuitive Monopoly game analogy:

"Suppose that, instead of rolling the die to get randomness, we decided to use a book of the digits of $\pi$. We start from a secret digit, and from that point each player uses the next digit in the sequence as their 'roll'. The moves are still indistinguishable from random rolls, but anyone who knows the sequence can verify after the game that $\pi$ was used."

When does watermarking abstain? Factual phrases (e.g. "Isaac Newton's Principia Mathematica") and rigid syntax (code) have near-zero entropy. Anthropic does not nudge these choices, ensuring factual fidelity.

The Cryptographic Sampling Theorem

Traditional watermarking approaches either altered the text (synonym injection, zero-width spaces) or biased logits with a fixed additive delta ($\delta$). However, fixed logit biases distort text quality.

Anthropic uses the non-distortionary tournament / Gumbel PRNG framework:

1. Context Window: $C_t = (x_{t-H}, \dots, x_{t-1})$
